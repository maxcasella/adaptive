Calisthenics · Harder by leverage

Leverage sets the load.

With no plates to add, you make a skill harder by moving your weight further from the shoulder. A straddle planche weighs the same as a tuck and is far harder, because the lever is longer.

  • Tuck plancheKnees to chest, the shortest lever
  • Straddle plancheLegs out wide, a longer lever
  • Full plancheBody straight, the longest lever

Calisthenics · Holds train an angle

A hold trains its angle.

Strength from a hold carries over most within roughly 20 degrees of the joint angle you trained. A tuck hold mostly builds tuck strength, so each step has to be trained in its own position.

  • HoldsStrength at the angle you hold
  • Slow negativesStrength through the range, lowering
  • Band-assisted repsThe full movement with less of your weight

Calisthenics · The common mistake

Chasing the muscle-up.

Most failed muscle-ups fail at the same point, the turnover, so each attempt rehearses the position you can't get through. What moves you forward is the pulling and pressing strength under it.
