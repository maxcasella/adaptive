Powerlifting · The nervous system

Strength starts in the brain.

Before a muscle grows, your nervous system learns to use more of it. That accounts for most of the strength you gain in your first months, and it keeps adding to your total for years after.

  • RecruitmentMore muscle fibers switch on for the same lift
  • Rate codingThose fibers get signals faster, so each one pulls harder
  • CoordinationThe muscles working against the lift learn to relax

Powerlifting · Why heavy is a skill

Heavy is a skill.

Those gains are specific. Near your max the bar moves slower, and the bracing, timing and positions change. They only improve with heavy, crisp sets that stop short of a grind.

  • Sets of 1 to 3 at RPE 8Rehearses bracing and bar path at meet weights
  • Sets of 8 to 12 at 60 to 70%Builds the muscle the heavy sets go on to use
  • Grinding to a maxTests the skill, and costs too much to do often

Powerlifting · The common mistake

Twelve weeks off one old max.

A percentage sheet assumes your max is current and every day is average. By week 6 the max is two months old and you slept five hours. 90% of the wrong number is the wrong weight.

Powerlifting · Meet day

Strongest on meet day.

Fatigue hides strength. In the last two weeks volume drops and the bar stays heavy, so the fatigue clears. Openers are weights you've already made.
