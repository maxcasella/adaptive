CrossFit · Three energy systems

Three ways to make energy.

Your muscles run on a molecule called ATP, and your body refills it three ways. How long and how hard you go decides which one does most of the work.

  • Phosphagen, up to 10 sFuel stored in the muscle, gone in seconds. It powers your heaviest lifts and sprints.
  • Glycolytic, 30 s to 2 minBurns sugar fast, without oxygen. It sets how long you can hold a hard pace.
  • Aerobic, 2 min and longerUses oxygen and lasts for hours. It also refills the other two between efforts.

CrossFit · The costly minutes

The burn costs the most.

Near all-out efforts of 30 seconds to 2 minutes can leave you flat for two days. The workout is short, so the cost is easy to miss until the next day's squat feels heavy.

CrossFit · The common mistake

Every day at max effort.

Five hard days in a row means most sessions start tired. The heavy lifting slips first, and the lift gets the blame instead of the workout the day before.
