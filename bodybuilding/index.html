Bodybuilding · How muscle grows

The last reps do the work.

Your body calls on small muscle fibers first and the biggest ones only as a set gets hard. Those big fibers have the most room to grow, so the reps near failure count most.

  • Size principleSmall fibers first, the biggest only close to failure
  • Mechanical tensionLoad on those working fibers is the signal to grow
  • Protein synthesisStays raised for a day or two after a session, then fades

Bodybuilding · Sets per muscle, per week

Count the week, not the day.

Growth tracks hard sets per muscle each week, about 10 to 20 for a muscle you're building. Past that the return shrinks. Split across two sessions, each set starts fresher.

Growth rises with weekly hard sets and levels off. 10 to 20 sets gives most of the gain, and past 20 the return shrinks.

Bodybuilding · The common mistake

One chest day a week.

After a session, the signal to build muscle lasts a day or two. Train chest once a week and it goes five days without it. The same sets over two days cut that to three.

Once a week, 16 sets

5 days off

Twice a week, 8 + 8 sets

3 days off

MTWTFSS

Bars show days the muscle-building signal is raised.

Once a week, the signal is raised on Monday and Tuesday only. Twice a week, it is raised on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday.
