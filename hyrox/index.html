HYROX · Running on tired legs

The run after the sled.

After a station your heart rate is close to max and your quads are short on oxygen. You then run 1 km on those legs, eight times. Your fresh 1 km time says little about how that goes.

HYROX · Where races are lost

Grip and sleds end races.

Running fitness gets you through the SkiErg and the row. The sleds, carries and lunges need leg strength and grip, yet most plans train neither.

The darker four are the sled push, sled pull, farmers carry and sandbag lunges.

The darker four are where grip and leg strength decide your time.

HYROX · The common mistake

A full sim every week.

A full simulation costs close to what a race costs and takes most of a week to absorb. Doing that weekly puts you at a disadvantage, causing times to drift up and strength work to lag.

Example
SimulationTimeChange
Week 11:24:40First
Week 21:25:15+0:35
Week 31:26:02+0:47
Week 41:27:30+1:28

Four full simulations in a row, each slower than the last.
