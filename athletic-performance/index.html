Athletes · Force, made fast

Speed is force, made fast.

A max squat takes two seconds or more. A sprint stride gives you about a tenth of one. Sport is decided by how much force you make in that window, called rate of force development.

Sprint stride
About 0.1 s
Jump takeoff
About 0.25 s
Max squat
2 s or more

Athletes · Stretch and rebound

Tendons give energy back.

When you land and jump straight back up, your tendons stretch and return some of that energy, so the second jump goes higher than one from a standstill. Plyometrics train that rebound.

  • Squat jumpFrom a pause, little rebound
  • Countermovement jumpA quick dip first, some rebound
  • Depth jumpDrop, land, jump, the most rebound

Athletes · The common mistake

One plan all year.

Off-season lifting is heavy and high in volume, perfect for weeks with no games. If that same lifting is continued once the season starts, you'll play tired, and your sprint times will lag come midseason.

Example
PhaseGamesLifting
Off-seasonNoneHeavy
Pre-seasonA fewHeavy
In-seasonEvery weekHeavy

The same lifting, with games on top.
