Hybrid · Two signals

Two signals, one muscle.

Lifting switches on mTOR, the signal that builds muscle protein. Hard or long cardio switches on AMPK, the signal that builds mitochondria. For a few hours after cardio, AMPK can turn mTOR down.

  • mTORSwitched on by lifting, builds muscle
  • AMPKSwitched on by cardio, builds mitochondria
  • Cardio right before liftingCan dampen the lifting signal for hours

Hybrid · Where the conflict is

The conflict is in the legs.

Running and squatting tire the same muscles, and running's impact adds damage on top. Upper body lifting barely notices your mileage, and cycling costs leg strength less than running does.

Bench after a run
Little effect
Squat after a ride
Some
Squat after a hard run
The most

Hybrid · The common mistake

Heavy squats after intervals.

Spread hard runs and heavy leg days across the whole week and your legs never get a day off. Your squat and your 5k both stall.
